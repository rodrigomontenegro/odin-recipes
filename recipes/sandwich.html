Sandwich

Description

Nothing beats a good ol' sandwich. This recipe is my favorite sandwich, which goes well as lunch, dinner or even as an afternoon treat.

Even when this recipe may seem simple, sandwiches are the kind of food where less means more.

Ingredients

  • Any kind of bread
  • Ham: 50 grams
  • Cheese: 50 grams
  • Pickles: 20 grams
  • Tomato: 40 grams
  • Lettuce: 2 leafs
  • Ketchup, mustard and a few drops of Tabasco sauce.
  • Grounded pepper
  • Paprika

Steps

  1. Toast your bread with butter in a pan
  2. Mix the ketchup, mustard and Tabasco sauce with a pinch of grounded pepper and paprika
  3. Put the sauce you made in both bread pieces
  4. Place the ham and cheese and grate in the oven at 200° for 10 minutes
  5. Place the pickles, tomato and lettuce
  6. Close with the top bread

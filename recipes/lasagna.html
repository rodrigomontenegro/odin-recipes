Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a very delicious plate you can easily make at home.

You can follow this recipe or spice things up and change the ingredients as you prefer!

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets: one pack
  • Minced meat: 500 grams
  • Two carrots
  • A pack of celery
  • 4 medium-sized onions
  • Salt, pepper and spices
  • 500 grams of tomato
  • 100 grams of grated cheese

Steps

  1. Cut the onion, carrots and celery
  2. Cook the vegetables in a pan until soft
  3. Add the minced meat and cook until golden brown
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 15 minutes
  5. Place the pasta sheets in the bottom of the baking dish
  6. Add a layer of the mix
  7. Repeat steps 5 and 6
  8. Top it with the grated cheese and bake 15 minutes at 200°C
